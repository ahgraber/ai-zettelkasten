# The Analytical Engine and Its Programmer

Charles Babbage designed the Analytical Engine as a general-purpose mechanical computer.
Ada Lovelace studied the design and saw further than Charles Babbage did.

## The Notes

Ada Lovelace published her notes on the Analytical Engine in 1843.
The notes describe how the Analytical Engine could weave algebraic patterns, and Ada Lovelace included a table for computing Bernoulli numbers.

## Legacy

Charles Babbage never completed the Analytical Engine.
Ada Lovelace is remembered for the first published program, and the Analytical Engine is remembered as its target.
